How Solar Works

Understanding the complete solar energy process

Step 1: Sunlight Absorption

Solar panels contain photovoltaic (PV) cells that absorb sunlight and convert it into direct current (DC) electricity. The more sunlight, the more electricity is generated.

Step 2: Inverter Conversion

The DC electricity generated by solar panels is sent to an inverter, which converts it to alternating current (AC) electricity that can be used by household appliances and the grid.

Step 3: Usage & Distribution

The AC electricity powers your home. Any excess electricity not used by your household can be sent to the electrical grid, earning you credits through net metering.

Step 4: Savings & Monitoring

Monitor your solar system's performance through our mobile app or web portal. Watch your energy bills decrease and savings increase month after month.

Solar System Components

Solar Panels

Convert sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic technology. Size ranges from 3kW to 10kW+ depending on your needs.

Inverter

Converts DC electricity to AC electricity that powers your home and can be exported to the grid.

Battery (Optional)

Stores excess solar energy for use during night or cloudy periods. Not required with net metering.

Monitoring System

Tracks real-time energy production and consumption with mobile app and web portal access.

Net Meter

Measures electricity sent to and received from the grid. Enables net metering benefits.

Safety Switches

Protection devices including DC isolator, AC breaker, and earthing system for safe operation.
