10 — Clear your doubts

Frequently
asked questions.

Everything you need to know about solar energy and our solutions.

Our solar systems reduce your electricity bills by up to 80%. Once installed, you get free electricity for 30+ years. Through net metering, you can earn credits for excess energy sent to the grid, providing additional savings and income.

You can avail government subsidies through PM Surya Ghar and other state schemes. We assist with all documentation and the subsidy application process. With subsidies up to ₹78,000, your installation cost becomes significantly more affordable.

Solar energy makes you independent from grid price fluctuations and power outages. With hybrid or off-grid systems, you gain complete energy autonomy. Our solutions provide reliable power generation for decades with minimal maintenance.

Solar installations increase your property value significantly. Our systems are designed for minimum maintenance with 30-year panel warranties and 10-year inverter warranties. We provide complete after-sales support for years to come.

We're rooted in Andhra Pradesh with 25+ years of experience. Our team provides end-to-end care from site survey to after-sales support. We use only high-quality components from trusted brands like Tata, Waaree, Solax, and RenewSys with full transparency in our recommendations.

Modern solar panels generate electricity even on cloudy days, though at reduced capacity. We design systems based on your annual consumption patterns. Hybrid systems with battery storage provide power even during poor weather conditions.
