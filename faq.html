Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to your solar energy questions

How much can I save with solar?

Most homeowners save 50-80% on their electricity bills. Your exact savings depend on your location, roof condition, and energy usage. We provide a free assessment to calculate your potential savings.

How long does installation take?

A typical residential installation takes 1-3 days. After installation, government approvals and net metering setup take an additional 2-4 weeks.

What is net metering?

Net metering allows you to send excess solar energy to the grid and receive credits on your electricity bill. This significantly increases your savings and ROI.

How long do solar panels last?

Solar panels typically last 25-30 years with minimal maintenance. Most panels come with a 25-30 year warranty covering performance and defects.

Do I need batteries?

Battery storage is optional. With net metering, you can feed excess power to the grid. Batteries are recommended if you experience frequent power outages or want complete off-grid capability.

What is the cost of solar installation?

Costs vary based on system size and quality. We offer flexible financing options including EMI plans with 0% interest. Government subsidies up to ₹78,000 are also available.

What maintenance is required?

Solar systems require minimal maintenance - just occasional cleaning of panels. Our team offers annual maintenance packages for optimal performance.

Can solar panels work during cloudy weather?

Yes, solar panels work even on cloudy days, though at reduced efficiency. They still generate 25-50% of capacity in low light conditions.

What is the process to apply for government subsidy?

We assist with the entire subsidy application process including PM Surya Ghar scheme. You can apply online at pm-suryaghar.gov.in or we can help you through the process.

Is solar installation disruptive?

Installation is minimally disruptive. Our trained technicians work efficiently to complete installation with minimal disruption to your daily routine.
